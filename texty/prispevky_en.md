**Description:**

From the Habsburg Monarchy to the Czech Republic: how the population of present-day Czechia changed between 1754 and 2021, told with just three simple pieces of data: the year, the population and the borders.

What the data show:
• The population kept growing during World War II and peaked around 1940 at over 11.1 million.
• The sharp drop came only after the war: between 1944 and 1947 the population fell by more than 2 million. Behind it are the victims of the war and the Holocaust, as well as the post-war expulsion of most of the German population. Human tragedies that affected everyone who lived here.
• The 2021 census counted fewer people (10.52 million) than the 1930 census (10.67 million).
• Since 2022 the population has been above the 1930 level again, mainly due to people arriving from Ukraine (10.91 million at the end of 2024).

All figures refer to the territory of present-day Czechia. Figures before 1869 are estimates.
